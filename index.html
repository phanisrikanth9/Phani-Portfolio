Family Safe Player

Watch movies with your family and skip the nudity and sex scenes. An image classifier scans the film inside your browser, turns its frame scores into scenes, and the player jumps past them before the first frame shows. Load the subtitles and it mutes swearing too.

Scene edges land within a quarter second, thanks to a second, finer pass around every cut. Nothing is ever uploaded.

TypeScript, React, TensorFlow.js in a Web Worker, Chrome extension for Netflix and YouTube

Stock Performance Data Pipeline

Every trading day a GitHub Action pulls prices for Tesla, Amazon, AMD and GameStop, models them in dbt on DuckDB, and publishes a dashboard where you can scrub through time and see what $100 became. There's also a SQL console that queries the warehouse in your browser.

30 data tests guard every publish. If one fails, nothing ships and yesterday's good version stays live.

Python, dbt, DuckDB, SQL, GitHub Actions, DuckDB-WASM

Visual Privacy Risk Detection

Drop in a screenshot or a photo of a document. It reads the text, finds card numbers, Aadhaar, PAN, SSN, phone numbers and passwords, and hands back a copy with them blacked out.

IDs are confirmed with their real checksums (Luhn, Verhoeff), so random digits don't trigger false alarms.

Python, fine-tuned MobileNetV2, OCR, scikit-learn, TensorFlow.js

Movie Recommendation System

Pick any of 4,800 films and get the ten closest matches, each with the reason it was picked: same director, shared cast, genres or themes.
